SHORTZ VS — EMULATOR ARCADE CREDITS
====================================

ENGINE
EmulatorJS 4.2.3 — open source (GPL), https://emulatorjs.org
License text: data-4.2.3/EMULATORJS-LICENSE.txt
Cores are the unmodified EmulatorJS builds (libretro cores: gambatte,
fceumm, snes9x [non-commercial license terms], genesis_plus_gx
[non-commercial license terms], mgba, stella2014, mednafen family,
mupen64plus_next, desmume, pcsx_rearmed, handy). Shortz VS is free;
no core is modified. No BIOS files are used or distributed.

GAMES
Every game in roms/ is freeware or open source, harvested title by
title under a written license gate. Per-game author, source, and
license basis: the harvest manifests in the project records
(emulator-harvest/MANIFEST.md, emulator-harvest-2/MANIFEST.md).
Each game's maker is also credited on its card in the Games tab.
